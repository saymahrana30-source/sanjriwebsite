Who We Are

A youth circle that shows up where help is needed

Sanjri Youth Foundation began as a small group of young volunteers in Gorwa, Vadodara, and has grown into an active youth circle running blood donation camps, free medical checkups, ration and clothing drives, and education support — almost always working alongside local trusts, mosques, and community partners.

Our Mission

Empower communities through education, women's development, social welfare, public safety support, and direct community service.

Our Vision

An inclusive, empowered society where every individual has access to opportunity and support.

We didn't start with funding or an office — we started with a handful of young people in Gorwa who decided that if no one else would organise a blood camp or stand at the Neki Ki Deewar handing out clothes, we would. Every camp, every drive, every rupee of ration we've given out since has come from that same decision to simply show up.
