Volunteer With Us

No paid staff, no office overhead — just volunteers from Gorwa and nearby areas showing up for each other. Here's how to join.

Flexible Hours

Most camps run on evenings and weekends — volunteer around your work or college schedule.

Real Hands-On Work

Set up camps, register donors, pack ration kits, or help run a vaccination drive — not just paperwork.

Grow Into Leadership

Many of our camp organisers started as first-time volunteers a few drives earlier.

How It Works

Three steps to your first camp

01
Fill the form below

02

03
